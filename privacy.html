Privacy policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Shorts Uploader ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted tool. It runs entirely on the computer or server of the person using it. The developer runs no servers for it and receives none of your data.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google) account: when you connect YouTube, the app asks for permission to upload videos to your channel (the youtube.upload scope). It uses this only to upload the videos you choose, with the title, description, tags and settings you choose.
  • Instagram and TikTok accounts: access tokens you provide, used only to publish the videos you choose.
  • Your videos and text: the files, titles and descriptions you add.

Where your data is stored

Sign-in tokens, settings, scheduled posts and videos are stored only on your own device or server, in the app's data folder. Nothing is sent to the developer, and the app has no analytics or tracking.

Who your data is shared with

  • YouTube / Google, when you upload to YouTube, and Google Gemini if you turn on AI summaries (a low-resolution copy of the video is sent to Gemini with your own API key).
  • Instagram (Meta) and TikTok, when you post there. To post to Instagram, the app may briefly make the video available at a temporary, random web link (a Cloudflare quick tunnel, or a temporary file host) so Instagram can download it; the link stops working shortly after.

Nothing is sold or shared with anyone else.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and deleting data

  • Disconnect any account in the app under Settings, which deletes its stored token.
  • Revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the app's data folder to remove everything it stored.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are posted on this page. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.

Shorts Uploader is a personal, open-source project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube, Google, Instagram, Meta or TikTok.
