Shorts Uploader

A personal tool for posting short vertical videos to YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok from one place.

Shorts Uploader runs on its owner's own computer or home server. You pick a video, write a title and description, and it posts the video to the accounts you've connected, right away or at a scheduled time.

  • Uploads to YouTube using the YouTube Data API (YouTube API Services), only to the channel you sign in with.
  • Posts to Instagram and TikTok through their official APIs.
  • Optional AI title, caption and tag suggestions with Google Gemini.

It only acts on accounts you connect yourself, and you can disconnect them at any time.
